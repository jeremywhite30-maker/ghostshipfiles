He Survived 76 Days Alone at Sea

SURVIVED

The dossier

Key facts

Date
Holed February 5, 1982. Picked up by fishermen in April 1982 on his 76th day adrift.
Location
Atlantic, about a week out from El Hierro, Canary Islands. Rescued off Marie-Galante, near Guadeloupe.
Vessel
Napoleon Solo, a 6.5 m sloop Callahan designed and built himself
Crew
Steven Callahan, alone
Cause
Holed in a night gale by an unknown object (Callahan suspected a whale)
Survival
76 days in a six-man inflatable raft about six feet across

Where it happened

Marie-Galante, near Guadeloupe, where fishermen picked him up (approximate). View larger map · Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Timeline

  1. Jan 29, 1982Departs El Hierro in the Canary Islands, bound for Antigua.
  2. Feb 5, 1982Holed and swamped in a gale. Watertight compartments keep the sloop afloat long enough for him to dive back in for gear.
  3. The driftSolar stills, a spear gun and a pencil sextant. Nine ships pass without seeing him.
  4. Day 76Birds circling the raft lead fishermen to him just off Marie-Galante.

Sources

  • Steven Callahan, Adrift: Seventy-Six Days Lost at Sea
  • Christian Science Monitor, Sept. 30, 1982
  • Mystic Seaport Museum pencil-sextant object record
  • Wikipedia: Adrift: Seventy-six Days Lost at Sea

Sources disagree on whether he was picked up on April 20 or 21, so we show the month only.

The story

76 days alone. A raft six feet across. The Atlantic had already counted him out. Then birds led fishermen to a man they were never supposed to find.

In 1982, Steven Callahan’s self-built sloop failed. He lasted 76 days in a six-foot raft until birds led rescuers to him off Marie-Galante. Captain Eight opens the file.
