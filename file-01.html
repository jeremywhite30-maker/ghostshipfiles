The story

On the morning of January 31, 1921, the five-masted schooner Carroll A. Deering was sighted aground on Diamond Shoals off Cape Hatteras. When she was boarded days later, the sails were still set, food was laid out as if a meal had been underway, and the crew was gone. The lifeboats were gone too. All 12 men were never seen again.

Pirates, mutiny, and the Bermuda Triangle have all been offered as theories. Five U.S. departments investigated. Nobody closed the case. Captain Eight opens File #1 on that open mystery.
