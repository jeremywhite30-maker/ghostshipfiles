The dossier

Key facts

Wreck
USS Samuel B. Roberts (DE-413), destroyer escort
Sunk
October 25, 1944, at the Battle off Samar
Location
Philippine Sea, off Samar
Crew
224 aboard. 89 killed in action, 25 more died later of wounds or exposure, 120 rescued.
Found
June 22, 2022, by Victor Vescovo's team
Depth
About 22,621 ft (6,895 m), the deepest shipwreck ever found

The story

The ocean just disappeared. You have until midnight. Captain Eight will walk you to the deepest shipwreck ever found — almost 7 kilometers down.

The walk crosses millions of wrecks, the Titanic, Shackleton’s Endurance, and Pacific war graves, then the pressure that should crush steel, and the ship that survived it: USS Samuel B. Roberts (DE-413), found in June 2022 at about 6,900 m.

The vanished ocean, the midnight deadline, and Captain Eight’s walk are fiction. The wreck facts carry a REAL tab in the video. Visuals are AI-generated illustrations. Many of these wrecks are war graves — please be respectful.
