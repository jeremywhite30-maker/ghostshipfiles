Lost at Sea for 76 Days on a 6-Foot Raft (True Survival Story)

The story

76 days. A 6-foot raft. 9 ships sailed right past him. His boat was sinking in the dark, so he swam back INTO it. That decision kept him alive.

In 1982, sailor and naval architect Steven Callahan was holed in a night gale about a week out from the Canary Islands. He dove back into his flooded sloop for gear, then drifted 76 days across the Atlantic in an inflatable raft about six feet across — solar stills, a short spear gun, a pencil sextant, nine ships that never stopped… and, in the end, birds.
